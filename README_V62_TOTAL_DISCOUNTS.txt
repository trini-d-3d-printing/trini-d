TRINI-D WEBSITE v62 / DESKTOP v3.0.8 — PDF discount totals
================================================================
All customer invoice, customer quotation, and available internal PDFs:
- "Discounts" = sum of per-item discounts ONLY.
- "Total Discounts" = sum of per-item discounts + the final overall discount.
- Total Discounts is a summary number, not an additional charge/deduction.
- Final payable total is still subtotal minus item discounts minus overall discount.
- Both rows appear when either type of discount is used; otherwise both are hidden
  on customer PDFs (internal summaries always show both).
- No changes to discount entry controls, pricing formulas or other PDF content.
